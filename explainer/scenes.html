HCP Targeting · Workflow
Four steps from strategy to the field
01
Targeting Strategy Alignment
  • Align on brand goals and priorities
  • Define the HCP universe and eligibility
  • Agree success KPIs up front
Outcome

Shared objectives and a defined HCP universe

02
Advanced Analytics & Opportunity Assessment
  • Collate sources in Dataiku and Python
  • Apply propensity and patient finder models
  • Rank HCPs by opportunity
Outcome

An opportunity-ranked HCP universe

03
Target List Optimization
  • Assign each HCP a tier from multiple attributes
  • Keep rep workload and capacity in balance
  • Plan call frequency by tier
Outcome

Tiered target lists with calls planned by tier

04
Field Enablement & Deployment
  • Load lists into Call Planning and Veeva
  • Equip the field with clear guidance
  • Monitor uptake and refresh
Outcome

Field-ready targets with clear guidance
